# Arrow 004 — pre-reveal research interpretation

This memo uses only in-sample (IS) policy results. Out-of-sample (OOS) remains the reused even-signal-month internal confirmation, not new independent validation. Profit and loss (PnL), drawdown (DD), end-of-day (EOD) and regular trading hours (RTH) refer to the modeled standalone accounts. The proposed claims are recorded before reveal in `cg_arrow004_selection.json`; this memo does not change any rule or score.

## Why no short finalist is forced

Qualified reserves are better than indiscriminately filling the same bench, but that does not establish an improvement over the original eight. R4 qualified reserves earn $8,679.64 versus $359.49 for blind reserves, yet trail S0 R4 by $2,922.36. R5 qualified reserves earn $9,114.10 versus $7,553.22 blind, yet trail S0 R5 by $3,972.59. Extra names and greater utilization add liabilities as well as opportunities. Primary/reserve incremental attribution is published separately rather than assigning all changed profit to the new names.

R4 confidence selection improves its normalized-eight comparator by $4,917.06, but produces only $8,534.34 with $25,410.56 DD. S0 R4 earns $11,602.00 with $21,721.52 DD. The one-formula average-exposure-matched fixed-ticket comparator earns $16,174.02 with $26,342.27 DD. Selection is therefore not established merely by beating a weak normalization parent. Removing the largest positive incremental symbol also erases its small positive selection increment.

The corrected carry policies do not earn their extra headroom. The initially attractive R4 carry score depended on an invalid full-budget first cohort; the half-budget repair changes it to -$2,410.48. The literal favorable-primary-plus-reserve extension earns only $1,231.80. R5 carry variants remain profitable but inferior to the strict qualified-reserve parent. These are closed failures of the tested utilization mechanisms, not unfinished queues.

Both fixed biweekly phases were tested. S0 R4 ranges from -$6,778.73 in phase A to +$26,844.12 in B; S0 R5 ranges from +$760.17 to +$24,570.05. A lucky start phase is not a new engine. The u=1.25 R5 control improves profit through larger allocations, while qualified reserves still trail its matching control. No realized OOS maximum was used for size calibration.

Most decisively, the separate causal prior-national-EOD reference sensitivity changes the short baselines to large losses. These later-session, incompletely declared price-unit references are not silently substituted into the primary convention, but their disagreement makes stale-mark short economics heavily data-limited. A clean accounting identity cannot settle a missing-price problem. Short confirmation therefore retains the two timing bridges and two common-budget anchors; it does not manufacture a new utilization winner.

## What the separate long laboratory learned

The economic mirrors are inadequate by themselves. L0 and L4 lose money. L5 earns $5,992.49, slightly less than its same-clock pre-order-gross-matched IWM benchmark. Merely normalizing L5 to a fuller portfolio raises profit and risk together. The recovery-normalized original eight earn $19,302.16 but sustain $41,311.33 DD and an approximately $135,328 minute-observed peak gross.

Recovery selection changes names: 91 of its 200 IS tickets come from original loser ranks 9–20, and membership overlap with the original-eight parent is about 37.5%. It earns $17,991.64, retaining 93.2% of parent profit while reducing DD about 19.8% and signal-month red losses substantially. Its frozen primary thesis is this combined observed-history/recovery-selection **ride tradeoff**. It does not claim a uniformly positive monthly profit increment: four of six signal-month increments are negative.

The observation-only ablation is a substantial challenge. Choosing the first eight loss-ranked names with observed required histories earns $22,596.21 with $35,856.87 DD. Recovery selection retains less than 85% of that ablation's profit, so it cannot claim the same ride improvement against it. Likewise, equal-dollar sizing of the identical recovery-selected names adds only $697.70 and is sensitive to individual contributors. Neither a small sizing gain nor exclusion of missing histories is mislabeled as proven recovery-ranking alpha.

The investigator-originated patient rule keeps at most eight fully observed favorable recovery names, each at strict B/8, and leaves other slots idle. It fills 88 tickets across 22 of 25 scheduled cohorts, averaging 3.52 fills per allocation and using about 44.2% of intended cohort budgets. Its $14,229.88 profit, $18,041.80 DD and $2,857.11 signal-month red-loss sum compare favorably with L5 at similar average gross ($24,809 versus $25,375). The primary balanced claim is explicitly against this similar-average-exposure anchor. Its fully allocated direct parent remains a separate comparator; the patient book retains only about 76% of that parent's profit, so no 85%-retention claim is made there.

Lower average exposure is not lower risk on every date. The patient book's minute-observed gross peak is about $98,168 versus $70,025 for L5, and its worst day is about 4.3% worse. Its largest EOD symbol fraction is similar, around 15.3% of equity. The common 20% rule controls new aggregate orders, not subsequent price drift. Higher peaks are reported within the common planning range, without fictional forced liquidation.

The patient high-volume allowance is not independently established. Eight such tickets contribute about $640, while 80 quiet/nonnegative-three-session-return tickets contribute about $13,590. Removing the high-volume allowance yields the PATIENT_MIRROR ablation: $14,013.66 profit and $16,804.10 DD. The actual ablation increment is only about $216 because changing one part of an equity-linked book also changes later quantities. Tier attribution and a recursively rescored ablation are different comparisons.

## Falsification and limits retained in the freeze

Both primary weekly claims survive the fixed-reference-equity comparison in IS; their apparent improvement is not solely compounding. The phase diagnostics are less reassuring. Recovery selection improves DD/profit retention in both phases, but B materially worsens its worst day. Patient phase A worsens all four tested downside dollar measures against phase-A L5; B improves several measures but worsens the worst day. Neither patient phase passes the same balanced claim. Only weekly policies are finalists, and all locked phase comparisons remain separate.

The bounded long alternatives include less-extreme losers, a single intermediate severity band, known-level reclaim, one-day floor stability, advancing-day counts, recovery-plus-counts, equal sizing, quiet actual recovery, a prior-mean reclaim, persistent participation and minute-bar directional recovery. Most fail to beat the relevant parent or IWM benchmark. The intermediate ranks 5–12 book loses $19,985.79. Persistent participation earns only $2,840.73, and minute-bar directional recovery $733.92. No later horizon, stop, discretionary exit, ticker exclusion or coefficient grid was opened to rescue them.

Within the already completed equal-dollar bottom-twenty book, favorable recovery tickets have only about a 0.12 percentage-point mean within-cohort unit-entry-gross return advantage over other tickets, positive in half of 22 comparable cohorts. Observed-versus-unknown-history differences are much larger. These descriptive comparisons do not randomize states or establish independent causal effects.

Both finalists exceed their IWM diagnostic in total IS profit, but the increment disappears when the strongest signal month is omitted. Removing their three largest positive symbol contributors also erases positive absolute profit. Patient-versus-L5 incremental profit is positive in five of six signal months and survives each leave-one-month-out arithmetic check; that more favorable parent comparison does not erase benchmark fragility.

The patient and selected books retain an unresolved WGS holding after the June 4 scheduled exit. Adverse missing-mark sensitivity lowers their results; the conditional national-EOD reference sensitivity raises them. Neither direction is promoted to a known executable result. Borrowing, financing, dividends, corporate actions and margin remain separate economic qualifications.

The freeze therefore carries two distinct **conditional research hypotheses**, not two deployable $100,000 accounts to be added together. The aim is to see whether their stated weekly tradeoffs repeat under the same comparator, costs, entry/exit clock and account engine. A failed confirmation will remain a failed claim; a favorable phase or secondary comparator will not replace it after reveal.
